# Launchpod 1.2.0

Collect multiple apps at the pointer and move them together across pages and folders.

## New

- In editing mode, hold `Cmd` and click apps to collect them into a stack. Release `Cmd` to place them in pickup order, with one Undo restoring the entire move.
- While carrying apps, switch pages with arrow keys, scrolling, page dots, or a pause at the screen edge. `Cmd`-click a folder to collect apps inside it, or release over a folder to add the whole stack.
- Pause over another app until it highlights to create a folder. Press `Esc` to cancel the collection.

## Fixed

- Safari and Passwords now appear in the app grid and search, including Safari installations exposed through a hidden symlink and standalone XPC app bundles.
- Icons keep wiggling after leaving an editing page and returning, including pages inside folders.
- The collected-app count is centered in its badge, and apps animate from the release position into their destination.
- Releasing `Cmd` during a page transition places apps on the destination page. Drops at page edges and over page dots also stay on the selected page.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac (arm64) running macOS 12.0 or later. Accessibility permission is required for trackpad gestures.

## Install

Download **Launchpod-1.2.0-arm64.dmg**, open it, and drag Launchpod to Applications. The app and disk image are Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple.

Existing users can choose **Check for Updates…** from the menu bar. For a Homebrew installation:

```sh
brew update
brew upgrade --cask elixirevo/tap/launchpod
```

For a new installation, use `brew install --cask elixirevo/tap/launchpod`.
